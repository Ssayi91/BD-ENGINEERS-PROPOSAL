Project Overview

Redesigning Your Digital Presence

The existing BD Engineers website will be redesigned and redeveloped to give the company a more current online presence and improve how its products, machinery and engineering solutions are presented.

The new website will retain the company's existing information and product range while improving the layout, navigation and overall presentation.

Enhanced Presentation

Improved layout, navigation and mobile-optimised design to ensure your engineering solutions are showcased professionally across all devices.

Expanded Functionality

Introduction of a dedicated news and blog section, downloadable product brochures and seamless integration with your official Facebook page.
